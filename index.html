Questions agents ask

Is PolicyCare really free?

Yes. You can create an account and use every feature at no cost.

Does it send WhatsApp reminders automatically?

Not automatically, because automatic messages need a paid WhatsApp Business service. Instead, PolicyCare shows who needs a call each day and writes the message for you, and one tap opens WhatsApp. You can also send yourself a daily summary.

Can I use it for many insurance companies?

Yes. Add policies of any company and any type, and search or filter them in one place.

Can I bring in my Excel data?

Yes. Use Import from Excel in the menu. PolicyCare matches your columns automatically and shows what will be imported before you confirm.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the website in your phone browser, and add it to your home screen to open it like an app.

Is my data safe?

Your data is stored under your own login and is not visible to other agents. Choose a strong password and keep it to yourself.
